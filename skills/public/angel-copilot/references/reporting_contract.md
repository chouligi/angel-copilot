# Assessment reporting contract (version 2)

Read this for JSON batch output and memo composition. The CLI prompt supplies a versioned JSON example. Empty assumptions and scenarios are allowed when the return model is unavailable. Never treat example numbers as a deal's facts.

## Memo reading order

1. **Decision Brief:** company/product/customer and market snapshot, stage/instrument/current terms, evidence date, INVEST with an exact normal cheque or WAIT/PASS with “No cheque recommended” (do not print a zero amount), decisive reason, four-part investment case and decision analysis, and next action. Include sizing only for INVEST and name whether a personal return hurdle exists.
2. **Seven-Factor Assessment:** compact table with category, effective weight, score, confidence and 8–15-word driver (hard limit 25 words). Put 40–70-word category notes across the page width. Cite evidence in place.
3. **Decision-Critical Diligence:** up to five ranked issues. Each has an evidence finding, consequence for the decision, evidence needed, and verifiable reconsideration condition. Each fact has one primary home. Merge risks, unknowns, reconciliation, upgrade conditions and milestones here; do not create separate repetitive lists.
4. **Top Questions for the Founder:** at most three concise, decision-changing asks. Three is a ceiling, not a quota. Link each to a diligence issue, request documents or a verification route when needed, and avoid multiple unrelated requests disguised as one question. Do not ask for already-supported facts. For a definite PASS, ask only if an answer could actually reopen the case; otherwise none.
5. **Deal Economics:** explicit assumptions and four net outcome stress tests. Show net cash outlay, ownership, fees/carry, loss probabilities and sensitivity with no follow-ons. If unavailable, explain why.
6. **Evidence Appendix:** optional follow-ups grouped by audience, dated source register, limitations, actual process status and concise input-document inventory. Questions for syndicate lead, counsel and customer belong here; they are not a founder outreach list. Keep source paths in JSON rather than printing long paths throughout the PDF.

Aim for 2–4 main memo pages per company, plus supporting evidence. An INVEST must have no unresolved investment-critical diligence conditions. Its questions should address residual monitoring/understanding, not work required before committing. In an archived report reconstruction, keep unranked old questions in the appendix; never call the first three “top” without prioritization.

## Structured fields

- `schema_version`: 2.
- `category_scores`: all seven keys, finite 0–5 or null. `category_confidence` rates evidence strength: high means key claims have independent authoritative corroboration; medium means some corroboration with material gaps; low means the available evidence is mostly reported, incomplete or conflicting; unknown means evidence quality cannot be assessed and is not a negative score. `category_drivers` and `category_rationales`: same keys.
- `deal_snapshot`: product, customer, stage, instrument, terms, market_timing; optional as_of and report_note.
- `decision`: verdict (INVEST/WAIT/PASS), reason, `assessment_summary` with exactly four parts—`investment_case`, `supporting_evidence`, `counterarguments`, and `decision_logic`—each 25–80 words and 140–260 words total. Distinguish verified evidence from claims, explain the strongest case against the recommendation, and state what could change the verdict. Economics (attractive/unattractive/unknown), evidence (sufficient/incomplete), fit (compatible/incompatible/unknown), suggested_amount (all-in cash; zero internally for WAIT/PASS, omitted from displayed cheque amounts), sizing_reason, next_action. Optional minimum_ticket must use the same all-in basis; convert a deployed-principal minimum before comparing it.
- `ticket_min`, `ticket_typical` and `ticket_max` live in the investor profile, not in the rubric. Without the complete min/max range, do not recommend or model a cheque; prompt for a complete profile range instead of substituting defaults.
- `diligence_issues`: up to five objects with unique id, integer priority (1 most important), status (fatal/blocker/risk/resolved), evidence_state (verified/reported/unknown/conflicting), title, finding, decision_impact, evidence_needed, reconsideration_condition, and source_ids. Founder's claims remain reported until the appropriate evidence verifies them.
- `questions`: question, priority, audience (founder/syndicate_lead/counsel/customer), optional (boolean), issue_id, evidence_requested. No more than three nonoptional questions, all for founder/company. Route SPV economics/rights questions to the syndicate manager. Optional follow-ups should be few and necessary, not a broad diligence checklist.
- `return_assumptions`: currency (investor currency), valuation_currency, fx_rate (valuation-currency units per investor-currency unit), entry_valuation, entry_valuation_basis (post_money), exit_value_basis (distributable_equity), fee_rate and dilution_rate as fractions, fee_treatment (deducted/added), carry_rate, carry_basis (deployed_capital/total_outlay), follow_on (false), years, ownership_note, exclusions. Both treatment choices use suggested_amount as total outlay: deducted deploys ticket × (1 − fee); added deploys ticket / (1 + fee).
- `return_scenarios`: exactly one kind each of loss, bear, base, upside, with scenario label, fractional probability, distributable-equity exit_value and concise rationale. Optional scenario dilution_rate or years may vary by outcome. Loss has zero recovery and positive probability. Do not put independently calculated multiples in place of these inputs; the CLI computes net values.
- `citations` / `web_sweep_sources`: id, source/title, source_type, date_published or date, date_accessed, url where applicable, and note. Use claim-linked IDs in prose. Dates not known are explicitly not recorded, not guessed.
- `assessment_process`: actual completion booleans (used_full_rubric, performed_web_sweep, reconciled_docs_with_web, built_return_model) and notes. Do not mark work completed without doing it. `assessment_limitations` explains unavailable material and how it affects judgment.

Legacy fields remain readable for provenance. Legacy reports are not silently upgraded to new decisions, ranked questions or verified net models.
